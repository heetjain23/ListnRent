# RentFit - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Overview
RentFit is a peer-to-peer ethnic wear rental platform where users can list and rent outfits.

---

## Problem
Ethnic wear is expensive and typically worn only once.

---

## Solution
A platform where:
- Users can list unused outfits
- Others can rent them for short-term use

---

## MVP Features
- Authentication (OTP-based)
- Listing creation
- Browse & rent outfits
- Deposit-based trust system
- Manual dispute handling

---

## Target Market
- Mumbai
- Age group: 18–30
- College students & young professionals

---

## Status
🚧 MVP in development
